KYC: Know Your Customer

KYC is a set of processes and procedures used by businesses, particularly in the financial sector, to verify the identity of their clients and assess their suitability to conduct business. The primary goal of KYC is to prevent financial crimes such as money laundering, terrorism financing, and identity theft.

1 Identity Verification

Confirming the customer's identity through government-issued documents like passports or ID cards.

2 Address Verification

Verifying the customer's residential or business address through utility bills or official documents.

3 Risk Assessment

Evaluating the customer's risk profile based on factors like their business activities and transaction patterns.

KYC Requirements

  • National ID Card or Passport (Front & Back)
  • Proof of Address (Utility Bill, Bank Statement)
  • Recent Photograph (Passport Size)
  • Signature on White Paper
  • Business Registration Documents (if applicable)
  • Tax Identification Number
  • Source of Income Documentation
